Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we get asked most.

What is this downloader?

TeraGrab is a tool that resolves public TeraBox share links so you can see and download the files inside, without opening the TeraBox app.

Do I need a TeraBox account?

No. You don't need an account, login, or the TeraBox app — only a public share link.

Is TeraGrab an official TeraBox service?

No. TeraGrab is an independent, third-party tool and isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to TeraBox or its owners. See the full disclaimer.

Which links are supported?

Public TeraBox share links, including password-protected shares if you provide the password.

Why is my link invalid?

The link may be incomplete, expired, private, or from a service TeraGrab doesn't support. Try copying the share link again from TeraBox.

Why can't I download a file?

Sometimes a share's direct link is temporarily unavailable on TeraBox's end. Waiting a moment and trying again usually resolves it.

Can I download a whole folder at once?

Yes — if a share contains multiple files, a Download all button appears so you can grab everything in one go.

Are my files stored on the server?

No. TeraGrab passes the download directly to your browser and doesn't keep permanent copies on its servers.

How long do download links remain valid?

Direct links come from TeraBox and can expire after a while. If a download stops working, just resolve the share link again.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes — TeraGrab works in any modern mobile browser on Android and iOS, no app install required.
